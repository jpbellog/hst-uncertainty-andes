# Results

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `mc_collision_bands_<variant>_present_<model>_hsb_cc_g13.csv` | Track–trench collision bands restored to the present-day margin, by chain (`ridge`), spreading partition (`pf`) and age: nominal latitude and longitude and the 2.5, 16, 50, 84 and 97.5 percentiles of latitude. Variants `total` (all sources) for the twelve models; `circuit`, `hotspot` and `wk08` (one source at a time) for the nine main-text models |
| `F5_model_comparison_numbers.csv` | Numbers behind Fig. 3 (model comparison) |
| `F4_deposit_distance_numbers.csv` | Deposit–track distances quoted in the text |
| `flat_slab_tracks_test.csv` | Table behind Fig. 5 |

They are written by `tables/make_present_bands.py`, `figures/make_F5_model_comparison.py`,
`figures/make_F4_deposit_distance.py` and `analysis/flat_slab_tracks.py`. When the figures are run,
the logs and some supplementary figures are also written here.
